# Ancient Hunts and expedition tactics

Ancient Hunt boss HP uses 80?100% of a tier budget according to the existing server-owned weapon and homunculus progression rating: Common 110, Mythic 135, Rare 165, Epic 205, Legendary 250. Guardians use their own template tier. Minion stats and all other combat multipliers remain unchanged. Global Bosses retain their previous HP calculation; already-created combat states retain their frozen HP. Offline Ancient Hunt previews use the minimum budget.

Guardian rosters and victories are permanent per player, zone, and parent. After every guardian is defeated, that player may start the parent boss outside its special rotation until the first verified victory. A loss or forfeit does not consume this opportunity. A zone-attributed verified boss victory is the durable consumption record, including victories from before this change. Afterward, rematches require the normal scheduled window. Historical wins without a zone cannot safely be attributed to a particular zone. Common cycles and Global Bosses are independent.

Expedition runs record consecutive right advances and reject a fourth before rewards or state change. The route DTO retains all three directions and adds allowed_directions and consecutive_right; old runs start with a zero counter. The selected encounter freezes the same difficulty tier used by victory rewards. Normal chooses among legal attacks; later tiers use progressively more of the existing tactical scoring, including lethal checks, status combinations, cleansing and defense awareness. Existing combats without a frozen tier keep their previous tactical behavior. Random ties use the existing deterministic combat draw.
